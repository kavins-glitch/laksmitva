Parth Gaur

Designated Partner

Parth Gaur is the Designated Partner and driving force behind Laksmitva Global Industries LLP, registered with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, India (DIN: 11060718). With a strong foundation in entrepreneurship and a forward-looking business vision, Parth has built Laksmitva Global Industries from the ground up into a diversified, multi-sector enterprise.

His philosophy centers on blending modern business strategy with technological innovation, creating ventures that are not just profitable but purposeful. From consumer brand development (Kavari Polo) to international trade and strategic consulting, Parth's leadership embodies the growth mindset at the heart of the company's culture.

Under his stewardship, Laksmitva Global Industries continues to expand its portfolio, forge strategic partnerships, and position itself as a credible player in both domestic and global markets.
